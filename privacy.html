Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026

Posty is a desktop application that runs on your own Windows computer. This policy explains what information Posty uses, why, where it is stored, and how you can remove it.

1. Information Posty uses

From TikTok, after you connect your account with TikTok Login Kit:

  • Basic profile (scope user.info.basic): your open ID, display name and avatar. Posty uses them to show which account is connected and where a video will be posted.
  • Posting (scopes video.publish and video.upload): permission to publish the videos you approve to your account, or to send them to your TikTok drafts. Before each post, Posty asks TikTok for your current posting options (for example the privacy choices available to you), so it can show them on the Post screen.
  • Your videos' statistics (scope video.list): the view, like, comment and share counts of your videos. Posty shows them in its Insights screen and uses them to learn which videos your audience prefers.

From you: the videos, captions, notes, ratings and settings you create in Posty, and any reference videos you choose to upload into it.

From other platforms you connect (Instagram, YouTube): the equivalent account details, publishing permissions and video statistics, under their own sign-in.

2. Where it is stored

  • Everything is stored locally on your computer, in Posty's data folder.
  • Platform access tokens are stored in Windows Credential Manager, not in plain files.
  • We don't run a server that receives your data. We don't sell, rent or share your data with advertisers.

3. Services Posty sends data to

  • The platforms you connect (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube): the videos, captions and post settings you approve, so they can be published, and the requests needed to read your profile and video statistics.
  • Anthropic (Claude): Posty uses Anthropic's Claude to plan videos, review video frames for mistakes, write captions, and analyze which videos performed well. For this, video frames, captions, notes and video statistics may be sent to Anthropic's API, under Anthropic's privacy policy. Your TikTok access token, avatar and other account credentials are never sent to Anthropic.

4. How long data is kept

Data stays on your computer until you delete it. Video statistics are kept so Posty can compare videos over time.

5. Your choices and deleting your data

  • Disconnect TikTok in Posty's Connections screen, or revoke Posty's access in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions). After you disconnect, Posty deletes its stored TikTok token and stops reading your TikTok data.
  • Delete local data by deleting Posty's data folder. That removes your videos, notes, statistics and settings from Posty.
  • To ask a question or request help removing data, email jkwiatkowski@student.neumont.edu.

6. Children

Posty is not intended for anyone under 18 and does not knowingly process children's data.

7. Security

Posty signs in to TikTok with the secure authorization flow TikTok requires for desktop apps (PKCE), keeps tokens in Windows Credential Manager, and only talks to the platforms through their official APIs over HTTPS.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed.
